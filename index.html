About Us

Family-Focused
Dental Care

Turner Family Dentistry was founded in September 1996 in a small building on Doctors Drive in Munford, Tennessee. In 2008, we built our current office on Tipton Street, with twice the space to better serve our growing family of patients. We've grown considerably, but our personal care has never changed.

  • Locally owned and operated since 1996
  • Latest dental technology and equipment
  • Comfortable, welcoming office environment
  • Walk-ins welcome
  • Caring, friendly staff that treats you like family
